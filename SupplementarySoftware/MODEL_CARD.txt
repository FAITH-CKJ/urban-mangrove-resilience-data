MODEL CARD — URBAN MANGROVE RESILIENCE

Study: Urbanization erodes resilience in global mangroves
Correspondence: Ruili Li (liruili@pkusz.edu.cn)
Purpose: annual ecological-state prediction within specified climate, sea-level
and urban-allocation scenarios; reconstruction of UMRI using fixed equations.

ECOLOGICAL FORECASTER
Architecture: five LightGBM annual-state-increment regressors and one habitat-
presence classifier. The regressors predict historical habitat retention,
connected habitat capacity, confirmed recovery, EVI and NDMI. Each ensemble has
180 estimators, 15 leaves, maximum depth 5, learning rate 0.035, minimum child
size 50, L2 regularization 3 and random seed 2100. Residual increments are scaled
by alpha=0.25 before physical-state projection. The presence gate is retained.

Inputs: 137 fitted features comprising a five-year state and mask history,
13 observed and target-year dynamic drivers, and nine static variables. Static
H and K_B geometry supports the physical projection. It is not an additional
fitted predictor. Missing observations retain explicit masks and are filled
causally from available history. Known habitat absence remains an observed state.

Data: 65,144 centres / 1,693,744 annual rows during 2000-2025. The 57,898 uniform
centres define evaluation support; 7,246 additional centres enrich rare training
states. Original providers and versions are in Supplementary Table 3. The
historical panel, derived from GMW v4.1.12 and the stated satellite/forcing data,
has 1,683,699 valid score rows, 30,178 known-absent rows and 63,164 missing state
values. Region-balanced whole-block weights are defined in Supplementary S6.

Splits: training targets 2006-2015, validation targets 2016-2020, final test
targets 2021-2025; ten complete spatial blocks are excluded during fitting for
validation/testing. Scaling and fitting weights use training support. Selection
compares LightGBM, GRU and Temporal Fusion Transformer candidates with persistence
on the fixed validation partition and a residual grid {0.25,0.50,0.75,1.00}.
Five- and ten-year recursive tests use the final 2021-2025 comparison period.
These are temporal and whole-block holdouts; repeated k-fold cross-validation
and a separately acquired external test dataset are not part of this design.

Evaluation: Supplementary Table 5 reports state MAE, regional additive-
score and UMRI MAE, direction accuracy and false absences, with persistence as
baseline. Temporal five-year S MAE is 0.008256 (persistence 0.007968); ten-year
terminal S MAE is 0.014260 (0.015376). Spatial values are 0.010121 (0.008235)
and 0.028363 (0.035941). UMRI MAE is lower than persistence in all four tests.
Regional direction and false-absence diagnostics are reported alongside errors.
The GMW source product's temporal filtering and geographic/sample support are
described in Supplementary Methods. No feature-ablation study or independent
validation of model interpretability was conducted. The exact UMRI component
decomposition explains the defined ecological score. The temporal and spatial
holdouts above evaluate the forecasting model's predictive performance.

Production: the selected design is refitted through 2025. The supplied central
fit and two region-stratified whole-block bootstrap fits (seeds 2101,2102) enter
15 equally weighted joint members per pathway, balanced across five GCMs and
three relative-sea-level quantile paths. Paired urban branches share the same
member assignment. The 17th-83rd percentiles summarize this scenario ensemble.
Scenario trajectories describe the specified forcings and allocation rules.
The model does not predict a complete future classified pixel map.

COASTAL LAND-COVER CLASSIFIER
The archived ensemble contains six class-specific MLPs and one all-class MLP.
Each has two hidden layers of 256 ReLU units and six output scores. The 17-band
feature recipe and checkpoint-specific subsets are in Supplementary Method S2.
The final decision rule starts with the all-class assignment and then applies
water, mangrove, tidal-flat and aquaculture overrides in that order. The archive
preserves the original train/test tables and seven checkpoint files. Those
archived partitions are distinct from the temporal/spatial forecaster split.

Classification input transfer was assessed on the same 3,857 usable archived
records using original and reconstructed inputs. The three-group accuracy for
reconstructed inputs is 91.0552%; the six-class accuracy is 76.5621%. The sample
contains 3,783 distinct pixel-years, including 11 pixel-years overlapping the
archived training set. Supplementary Method S2 and Source Data report all exclusions,
duplicate records and the original-input comparison. These data evaluate input
transfer on an archived labelled sample. The original-feature training recipe
is retained for classification; harmonized reflectance supplies canopy indices.

REPRODUCIBILITY AND ACCESS
The software package contains source code, three ecological checkpoints, seven
land-cover checkpoints, real forecast inputs and expected outputs, classifier
sample tables, versioned requirements and installation/run instructions.
Full geospatial production uses the source archives listed in Methods. The
curated Source Data dataset is deposited at
https://github.com/FAITH-CKJ/urban-mangrove-resilience-data.
The complete dataset is openly available under CC BY 4.0, without registration,
to enable verification, reuse and further research. The accompanying Source
Data attachment contains the values underlying the displayed summaries,
individual distribution and scatter values, and annual time points. Its
figure-table index links to the complete numerical records and larger
spatial inputs in the repository.
Hardware, tested versions, demo runtime and licences are in README.txt and
DEMO_RUNTIME.txt. Checkpoints are trusted study artefacts and should be loaded
only from their supplied, checksum-verified files.
