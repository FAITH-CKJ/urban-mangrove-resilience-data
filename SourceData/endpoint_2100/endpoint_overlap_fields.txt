Spatial overlap of projected resilience deficits and additional urban loss in 2100

The historical reference comprises 934,814 distinct 30-m equal-area cells
(841.3326 km2). Forecast support contains 926,220 cells; 8,594 remain unknown.
The matched2025 companion contains the 925,774 forecast cells with valid
historical UMRI in 2025. All quantities use the UMRI definition
V = S/(lambda + abs(S)), lambda = 0.05934161890467785.

pixelwise_median_endpoints_2100.npz contains the numerical observations:

blocks: labels for 141 spatial processing blocks.
block_index: zero-based index into blocks for each reference cell.
centre_index: the cell identifier within its processing block.
The pair (blocks[block_index], centre_index) uniquely identifies every cell.
region_id: region 1-10, following supplementary/regional_population.csv.
valid: common validity across all forecast members and urban branches.
matched2025: valid forecast cell with a valid historical UMRI in 2025.
scenarios: ssp126, ssp245, ssp370, ssp585, in array-row order.
growth_median_UMRI: within-cell median of the 15 growth-branch UMRI states.
urban_median_delta_UMRI: within-cell median of the 15 paired growth-minus-
fixed2025 UMRI differences. Pairing precedes the median.
state_negative_votes: number of members with negative growth-branch UMRI.
urban_erosion_votes: number of members with negative growth-minus-fixed2025
UMRI difference. Eight or more votes correspond to a negative median.

State, difference and vote arrays have shape (4 scenarios, 934814 cells).
Unknown states and differences are NaN. Votes at unknown cells do not define
an ecological class; the validity mask is applied before counting.
Scores are dimensionless final UMRI values, with states in (-1,1) and
differences in (-2,2). The supplied values retain the precision of the
archived endpoint medians. Exact stored zero is retained separately from NaN.

state_and_urban_loss_overlap_2100.csv summarizes the same cells:

scenario: pathway identifier.
support: forecast or matched2025, as defined above.
region_id: 0 denotes all regions pooled; 1-10 denotes a single region.
reference_n: all historical reference cells in the stated region.
valid_n: cells evaluated on the stated support.
unknown_n: reference_n minus valid_n on that support.
negative_state_n: cells with growth_median_UMRI < 0.
additional_urban_loss_n: cells with urban_median_delta_UMRI < 0.
negative_state_and_urban_loss_n: cells satisfying both inequalities.
nonnegative_state_and_urban_loss_n: cells with additional urban loss and a
nonnegative projected state.
negative_state_without_urban_loss_n: cells with a negative projected state
and a nonnegative urban difference.
joint_area_km2: negative_state_and_urban_loss_n multiplied by 0.0009 km2.
joint_pct_reference: 100 times the joint count divided by reference_n.
joint_pct_additional_urban_loss: 100 times the joint count divided by
additional_urban_loss_n.
joint_pct_negative_state: 100 times the joint count divided by negative_state_n.
An undefined ratio with a zero denominator is blank, not zero.

The main text and Supplementary Table 9 use support=forecast, region_id=0.
The overlap describes spatial coincidence of a negative projected state and
an additional loss due to urban expansion at the same 2100 reference cell.
The urban difference compares development choices under common future
climate, relative sea level and ecological-model conditions.

These are areas of distinct historical reference cells classified by the
500-m-neighbourhood index. Overlapping neighbourhood areas are not summed,
and the reference area is not the surviving mangrove area in 2100.

Reproduction:
python SupplementarySoftware/code/derive_endpoint_overlap.py

The script reproduces the negative-state and additional-loss counts in
pixelwise_median_negative_area_2100.csv, verifies the regional sums, and
reproduces every overlap count and percentage from the supplied cell arrays.
