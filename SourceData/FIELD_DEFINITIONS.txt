Units and spatial populations

The final UMRI score is S/(lambda+abs(S)), with lambda=0.05934161890467785.
All reported UMRI states, UMRI changes, UMRI component contributions and UMRI
prediction errors use this common dimensionless scale. States lie in (-1,1); paired differences can lie
in (-2,2). Cumulative effects are expressed in UMRI-years. Retention m, connected capacity C, recovered habitat r
and urban pressure p are dimensionless. Multiplication by 100 expresses
percentages, and their changes are percentage points. EVI and NDMI differences
retain their original dimensionless index units.

The historical reference comprises 934,814 equal-area centres, each representing
900 m2, for a total of 841.3326 km2. Full historical estimates use the valid
observations in each year. The forecast population contains 926,220 centres
valid across all scenario members and urban branches. The matched2025 population
contains the 925,774 forecast centres with valid observed scores in 2025;
Fig. 2b-l uses this population for both periods and retains annual validity.
region_id 0 denotes all regions pooled; region_id 1-10 follows the population
table. scheme pooled_centres denotes the primary equal-area estimator.

The endpoint trajectory sensitivity contains 925,579 centres drawn from the
matched2025 population with valid scores in both 2000 and 2025. The full
historical comparison of habitat states contains 925,583 centres observed at
both endpoints. Patch concentration q=C/m additionally requires positive
retention in both years, giving 921,921 centres. The complete-history
population contains 876,841 centres, of which 874,224 have valid endpoint q.
Each endpoint difference uses the same centres in its two years.

Known complete habitat absence contributes zero retention, capacity and
recovered stock. Canopy observations retain their presence and observation
masks. Blank and NaN values denote missing or undefined quantities according
to the stated field definitions. A zero construction target has zero amounts
and an undefined allocation percentage.

Aggregation and scenario comparisons

UMRI components and the compensation factor are evaluated at each centre.
Regional and overall estimates average their weighted sum S before applying
the fixed normalization. The overall mean weights each valid historical
reference cell equally. Alternative equal-region and equal-block estimates
are identified by their scheme fields.

The urban contrast is growth minus fixed2025; the protection contrast is
protected minus growth. Branches are paired within the same climate,
sea-level and ecological-model member. Quantiles summarize those paired
differences. The 17th-83rd percentiles describe the range among 15 joint
members. Cumulative effects sum annual differences from 2026 to 2100 within
each member before calculating quantiles. Avoided-loss fractions likewise
divide protection benefit by additional urban loss within each member.

State components sum to the corresponding UMRI state. Change components use
the common secant of the fixed normalization and sum to the final UMRI change. Historical changes use 2000 as their comparison year. Future mean
component contributions are averages of within-member decompositions.
The mean contribution share and the median total effect have distinct
ensemble definitions. methods/umri_reporting.json defines the final-score
fields. S and fields named *_contribution are weighted computational
intermediates; *_state_UMRI and *_change_UMRI are the reported final-score
components. Their sums equal the corresponding UMRI state or change.

Urban construction is in km2. Each year records cumulative additions since
2025. The protected-branch target is the construction realized in the
corresponding unprotected growth branch. Within-source-cell construction,
nearby relocation and unserved growth sum to that target. Construction
fractions pool amounts before division and therefore weight source cells
by their construction targets.

Individual neighbourhood observations

figure_story/individual_neighbourhoods_2025.parquet has 925,801 rows. The pair
block_id + centre_index identifies each reference neighbourhood. region_id
identifies one of the ten regions in supplementary/regional_population.csv.
complete_history is true for 876,841 centres observed throughout 2000-2025.
m_2025: fraction of historical habitat occupied by mangroves in 2025.
C_2025: connected capacity in 2025, on the dimensionless [0,1] scale.
C_baseline: local 2000-2004 baseline connected capacity.
r_2025: fraction of historical habitat with confirmed recovery in 2025.
r_2025_pct: 100*r_2025, in percent.
EVI_2025 and EVI_baseline: dimensionless current and baseline greenness.
EVI_delta: current minus baseline EVI.
NDMI_delta: current minus baseline moisture index, dimensionless.
g_2025: signed canopy-greenness response used by the UMRI definition.
T_2025: structural trajectory (C_2025-C_baseline)/(C_2025+C_baseline),
with zero assigned when both capacities are zero.
Missing canopy differences at the 3,658 centres with known mangrove absence
remain missing. Habitat absence retains its measured zero habitat state.
All original observed values are retained at their stored precision.

File formats

CSV files use UTF-8, one header row, commas, decimal points and empty fields
for missing or undefined values. JSON files describe parameters and definitions.
Parquet files contain typed tables; use pandas.read_parquet or an equivalent
Apache Parquet reader. NPZ files contain named NumPy arrays and can be read
with numpy.load(path, allow_pickle=False). GeoPackage/GeoJSON files contain
spatial definitions. CRS and grid conventions are given in urban_coverage/.
FIELD_SCHEMA.csv lists the columns and array names in each numerical file.
FIGURE_TABLE_INDEX.csv maps all figures and tables to their source files.
MANIFEST.json provides file sizes and SHA-256 digests.
